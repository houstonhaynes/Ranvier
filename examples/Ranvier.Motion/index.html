Motion that belongs to the view.

Each page below is an F# component tree. Its animations are created when an element is placed, reverted when its owner is disposed, driven by signals and published back as signals. Lists and arms enter, leave and reorder with FLIP, and the story page is rendered on .NET and hydrated in the browser.

  • Kanban

    An Elmish board: presence on every card, FLIP across lanes, drag to drop, undo.

    forEach · presence · flip · drag

  • Dashboard

    Live metrics from a core stream: following gauges, a drawn and morphing line chart, tweened numbers.

    follow · draw · morph · track · boundary · fade

  • Story

    A scroll-driven landing page rendered by the .NET server and hydrated strictly in the browser.

    scroll · split · stagger · appear · SSR

  • Physics

    A spring playground: a thrown puck, a chain of followers, a scrubbed timeline and the motion policy.

    drag · spring · follow · scrub · policy

  • Routes

    Hash routing over page arms that cross-fade with pinned exits, each page with its own Elmish loop.

    matchWith · presence · Mvu · flip
