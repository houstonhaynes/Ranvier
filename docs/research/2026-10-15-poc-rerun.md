## What moved

- **swap1k:** 4.556× → 2.202× at the median, which meets ≤ 3×. The per-process ratios split with Solid 2's two modes (1.96–2.38 and 6.37–6.88), so the target is met only in the processes where Solid 2 runs its slower mode. Against Solid 2's fast mode the ratio is 6.37–6.88; production's median is 0.62 ms at `4353219` and 0.91 ms here.
- **remove1k:** 5.000× → 6.833×; ≤ 3× is not met. Solid 2 is 0.12 ms on both days; production is 0.59 → 0.81 ms. `remove10k` moved the same way, 11.0× → 13.3×.
- **clear1k:** 1.607× → 2.273×, now over ≤ 2.0×. In this run Solid 2's clear1k is 0.61 ms against 0.85 ms at `4353219`, while production is 1.34 → 1.36 ms.
- **update10th1k:** 0.210× → 0.127×.
- **create1k:** 1.690× → 1.809×, still within ≤ 2.0×. Append, replace, create10k, armsCreate1k, armsSwitch1k and armsPayload1k are within 0.09 of their earlier ratios; armsClear1k moved 2.333× → 2.489× and clear10k 2.561× → 2.801×.
- **Retained heap:** unchanged within 4 bytes per row by snapshot diff (1,227.8 → 1,224.6).
- **Components:** mount and update ratios against Solid 2 are within 0.05 of `a0de558` at every width. Disposal moved 1.56× → 1.39× at 128 rows, 1.25× → 1.33× at 16 rows and 1.32× → 1.43× at one row. Against Ripple, the 128-row mount is 0.74× (0.81× before).
